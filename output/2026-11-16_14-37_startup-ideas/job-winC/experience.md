# Experience: SideProjectors

- Homepage https://www.sideprojectors.com/ renders the project list directly (no login needed). Cards are links to /project/<id>/<slug>.
- Each card contains: price/stage label (e.g. "For sale ~ $1,200 negotiable", "Showcase", "Seeking Co-Founder / Collaborator"), title, one-line description, seller profile link.
- Sponsored cards ("From our sponsor") are mixed in; skip them.
- Working method: one navigate, short wait (3s), snapshot, read cards. No scrolling or pagination needed for 30 items.
- Not verified: pages beyond 1 (pagination shows 451 pages), grid view, individual project pages (category not checked there).
